# Solution Outline Section Guidelines

## Source priority
1. Objectives
2. Validated diagrams
3. Requirements Inventory only during final review

## Section population rules
- Populate a section only if supported by Objectives or diagrams.
- Omit unsupported sections entirely.
- Use diagrams to reinforce architectural structure and relationships.
- Use Objectives to explain scope, intent, assumptions, and constraints.

## Content rules
- Keep content architectural, not implementation-specific.
- Refer to diagrams explicitly where relevant.
- Maintain terminology consistency with Objectives and diagrams.
- Do not add technology details unless already present in authoritative inputs.
