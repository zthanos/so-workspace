# ADR Evaluation Rules

Evaluation should check for:

## Missing context
Decision lacks explanation of architectural drivers.

## Unclear decision
Decision statement is vague or ambiguous.

## Missing alternatives
Alternative approaches are not documented.

## Missing consequences
Trade-offs and impacts are not explained.

## Inconsistency
ADR conflicts with diagrams or Solution Outline.

## Terminology mismatch
ADR terminology differs from architecture artifacts.
