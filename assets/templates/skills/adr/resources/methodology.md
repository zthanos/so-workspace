# ADR Methodology

Architecture Decision Records capture important architectural decisions
made during the design of the system.

ADRs document:

- the decision
- the context
- the alternatives
- the consequences

The purpose is to preserve architectural reasoning and enable future
engineers to understand why the architecture evolved as it did.

ADRs must be:

- concise
- decision-focused
- traceable to architecture artifacts

They must not:

- repeat the entire Solution Outline
- include unnecessary implementation detail
- invent unsupported requirements or systems

Each ADR follows this lifecycle:

Generate → Evaluate → Patch → Recheck
