# Objectives Methodology

The Objectives document is the architectural interpretation of the validated Requirements Inventory.

Its purpose is to:
- translate requirements into architectural intent
- establish the scope and direction of the Solution Outline
- provide a stable bridge between requirements and architecture
- prepare the basis for Solution Outline, Diagrams, and ADRs

The Objectives document must remain:
- strictly derived from the Requirements Inventory
- implementation-neutral
- business and architecture oriented
- descriptive rather than overly bullet-based
- free from unsupported scope or inferred design

The Objectives document must not:
- introduce technologies, products, or implementation details
- resolve ambiguities not already grounded in the inventory
- invent new capabilities, systems, or constraints
- bypass requirements traceability

The document should express:
- business context
- scope
- stakeholders
- logical systems involved
- functional and non-functional objectives
- high-level flows
- integrations and data movement intent
- assumptions, constraints, risks, and open questions
